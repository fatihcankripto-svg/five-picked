# Editorial brand identifiers

These are small identifiers alongside independent product descriptions, not endorsement badges or FIVE PICKED branding. All 45 displayed files were retrieved from the product’s official website or a CDN URL explicitly published by that site. Source URLs, retrieval dates, file hashes and omitted entries are recorded in sources.json.

Provenance is not a blanket licence. Brand owners retain their rights. The source SVG/PNG/JPEG files are not recoloured, cropped, rotated or redrawn. ICO files are decoded to PNG without changing their design. All display uses preserve aspect ratio and clear space.

Known constraints reviewed:
- Adobe: product icons and logos have separate rights; omitted. https://www.adobe.com/legal/permissions/trademarks.html
- Hostinger: permission or affiliate-supplied assets are required; no permitted asset evidenced here, so omitted. https://www.hostinger.com/legal/brand-guidelines-permissions
- Dropbox: commercial use requires checking with Dropbox; omitted. https://www.dropbox.com/branding
- monday.com: the full coloured logo with monday.com is required, not capsules alone; no suitable full asset retrieved, so omitted. https://monday.com/p/news/press-kit/
- ClickUp: use the current official asset without alteration or implied endorsement. https://clickup.com/brand/trademark-guidelines

45 of the 90 published tools have displayed assets. The remaining names stay visible with no invented substitute logos. Some omissions are technical retrieval failures, not legal findings. This record does not claim a full legal clearance of all 90 brands or a guarantee across jurisdictions.

Assets are served locally. Page views make no third-party logo-service requests. Do not replace this registry with a guessed-domain image service.
